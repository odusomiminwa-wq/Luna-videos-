# yt-comment
Read ~/.codex/youtube/voice.md if it exists. Triage comments into questions, corrections, praise, and bait. Questions get answers; corrections get checked; praise gets specific brief replies; bait gets no reply. Keep replies under 30 words. Choose one comment to pin. Nothing here publishes.
